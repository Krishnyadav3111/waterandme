  2. In the memo, write your full name, your email, and the plan (Drop-in, Unlimited, or 8 classes).

Questions

Do I need experience?

No. Beginner classes are marked on the schedule.

What do I need?

A yoga mat, a quiet space, and Zoom on any device.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Just don't send next month's payment. You keep access until the end of the month you paid for.

Who can join?

Adults 18 and over. Zelle requires a US bank account.
